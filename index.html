About This Map

Data sources

  • Redlining: Home Owners’ Loan Corporation maps (drawn 1935–1940) from Mapping Inequality (Robert K. Nelson, LaDale Winling, et al., University of Richmond; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), via its census-tract crosswalk, dissolved back to one polygon per HOLC area. Exact map years are given where verified (New York City 1938, Houston 1937); elsewhere the map shows “1930s”.
  • Income, poverty, race & ethnicity: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2018–2022 5-year estimates, by tract. Suppressed or unreliable estimates show as “No data”.
  • Toxics Release Inventory: EPA TRI facility registry (locations only), clipped to each city’s map area.
  • Featured cities (New York City, Houston): curated highway histories and timelines with a source on each item; FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (Houston); political districts from Census TIGERweb and city/county sources, officeholders as of Sept 27, 2026.

Census layers show a single recent period. HOLC maps recorded lending judgments that the FHA and private lenders were already applying; historians debate how much the maps themselves caused. Some places lack Census layers (for example, Connecticut cities, where the Census Bureau replaced counties with planning regions in 2022).
