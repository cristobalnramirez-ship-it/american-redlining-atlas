About This Map

Data Sources

  • Redlining: HOLC maps digitized by the Mapping Inequality project
  • Highways: Historical records of highway construction and community displacement
  • Income & Demographics: US Census Bureau American Community Survey
  • Flood Zones: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer
  • Toxic Releases: EPA Toxics Release Inventory
  • Political Districts: Census TIGERweb

Income and race 2020 values use real ACS 2022 5-Year data. Historical decades (1970–2010) are modeled estimates. Featured cities include additional curated layers (highways, political districts, timeline narratives).
